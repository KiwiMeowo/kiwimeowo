Worlds UtSS Worldbuilding

Humans

"The inhabitants of this world, the wielders of magic."

The people of this land is a bit unlike ordinary humans, they are not made of flesh and blood, but magic essence. In fact, all life beings are made with magic. When magic condenses onto leaves or grass, they form into tiny gems, which may become sentient. When they grow larger, they may become animals or humans. Depending on the type of gems formed, the beings will have different magic types. When a child is found, a mentor should take care of them. Humans have high magic capacity and abilities than animals, and able to learn around three unique skills.

The Hero

"Legend has it that when the world falls into disarray, a Hero will rise and guides the world back into its glory days. With her love, friendship and justice, she shall defeat the evil and restore the hope in people's hearts."

It was never mentioned who the Hero actually is, or what the Hero looked like. However, with an appearance of a figure, the people all believed it has the be that person: Mira. Because of her many accomplishments in a short period of time, many regarded her as the one who will finally solve the problem of the Void.
