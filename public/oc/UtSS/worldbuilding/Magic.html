Magic

"Magic is how connects us all together, is it not?"

Base elements

  • Nature: Magic connected to plants and animals. Also, those with Nature Magic tend to be physically strong and peaceful. Powers include enhanced plant growth and animal-like senses.
  • Water: Magic related to manipulating water. It is said this type of magic users are quite sensitive to other's emotions, and knows how to bond with everyone. Powers include crystal-forming, water control, lightning.
  • Energy: Magic which controls heat and energy. This magic can be very destructive and powerful, but low in defense. It is said that these users are quite hot headed. Powers include fire, heat,

Complimentary elements

What's special about Complimentary elements is not only they are rare, they are also mainly to enhance other people's magic powers, which means it is ideal for Complimentary users to use their powers with a Base user. Perhaps Complimentary users might perform well in a team?

  • Light: Associated with creation and healing, it is said these users have justice in their hearts. Powers include item summoning, healing.
  • Dark: Associated with destruction and innovation, these users are often seen to be unafraid of new ideas, and is always willing to challenge the rules. Powers include skill inversion, magic nullify.

Method of Magic

The most common method of magic usage is convert magic essence from one's body into external power, this is called internal magic. There are basic magic skills everyone should be able to learn according to their own magic types. But if they became experts, they may find powerful abilities that they themselves can only master.

Aside from directly using your own magic essence, there are alternatives, which is external magic. You can extract magic from other matters or life beings, and apply it onto weapons or contain it. You can also draw magic circles to draw magic from surronding areas, although this is more time-consuming and requires more drawing skills. These can help you boost your powers or help you out if you are out of juice.
