The Void

"Plants rot, creatures die, life would fail to thrive."

The Void is the black substance growing on plant matters, or in some cases, on animals and humans. It is a highly toxic material that absorbs magic essence from lifeforms, weakening them and potentially killing them. There is no known way to retrieve the lost energy from the Void, or methods to destroy them.

The Void can also spread via contact or food consumption, therefore people are discouraged from approaching the Void and those infected by it. While treatment may be feasible on the early stages of Void infection, it is difficult to detect, which may lead to late diagnosis and a higher fatality rate.
