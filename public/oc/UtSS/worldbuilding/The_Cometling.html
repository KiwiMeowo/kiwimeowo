Humans

"I am Cosmos, the Cometling that will be your companion from today on!"

A Cometling is a small creature with wings and are able to communicate with humans. Unlike humans and animals, they cannot use magic, but they have ginormous magic energy inside them. According to records, there is only one instance of Cometlings.

Appearance

In their low energy form, they will appear formless like a wisp of fire, but contact with them is possible. Under this form, most detailed patterns originally on their body will disappear, their body will contain only a single color.

In the high energy form, they would appear as a creature similar to animals, except they may have unnaturally colorful patterns and have a pair of wings on their back.

Comet Guardian

When a human and a cometling share their powers via a Bond, they are able to use unstoppable magic via transformation, turning into a Comet Guardian. Not only does the Cometling allow for extra magic capacity, they also may have different magic element from the human's, allowing the two to use two distinct elements at once.

To cancel the Bond, the Cometling would have to separate from the human from a far away distance. However, it is not yet known how far away it has to be.
