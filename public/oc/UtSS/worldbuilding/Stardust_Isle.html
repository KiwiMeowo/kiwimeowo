Voided Lands

Stardust Isle used to be a lot larger. However, since long ago, the parts of the earth was consumed by some sort of pure black substance, referred as the Void. Any efforts of removing it has been futile, and all life that came near it quickly got ill. With no choice, the areas consumed by the void was barred from entering. As years go by, the Voided Lands kept increasing in size, and with the population quickly declining, all prohibition from entering and recovering has ceased. But perhaps, there are secrets laying inside of this area?

The Tower of the Divine

It is said that this tower, which is located at the center of the Stardust Isle, was once the home to the creator of this world. But in the current days, it is maintained by a team called The Sky Pioneer who promised to save the world from doom and bring back to glory days. However, ever since Mira passed away, the actions of this team has been more secretive than ever. Just what are they planning?

Communities

As you know human families don’t work the same here, people live in a community/village which is similar to academies. There is the leader, many mentors that will take care and educate the younger ones, and the students. Depending on the community, they will accept students of different magic types or capabilities.
