Quest for a better Ipad art program (ft Medibang Paletta and Adobe Fresca)

An underrated art program? Or a waste of money?

In the blog of 21 May 2026, I mentioned that I have switched to Ipad for drawing to save my physical space. While things are mostly well, I am somewhat bothered by the art program I'm using: Medibang Paint, therefore I have been looking for some alternatives, even if it costs money.

To explain what kind of complaints I have regarding Medibang, here's a short list:

  • Too many ads if you have wifi on
  • It sometimes crashes when I add materials
  • Bad UI

While the issues are mostly ignorable, I kind of want to turn on wifi to stream on Discord, I wanted to chat with my friends while drawing. Moreover, I'm kinda sick of having to toggle wifi everytime I need to share my art, it's like:

  1. Export art
  2. Turn on wifi to airdrop art
  3. Realised there's some mistake in the art
  4. Goes back to Mediibang
  5. Ads pop up because I forget to turn off wifi
  6. Close app
  7. Turn off wifi
  8. Open app again to draw

While I CAN live with it, I don't really mind spending money to reduce some of that annoyance. However, I would draw the line on subscription-based stuff, so no Medibang Premium, no IbisPaint premium, no Clip Studio Paint on Ipad.

Procreate

When it comes to drawing apps on Ipad, Procreate first comes to mind. It might be the most popular option, but of course, it costs HK$98, not the most expensive but it costs money. And sadly, there is no demo version to draw with, so I have no way to test out whether it is a good choice.

According to online reviews, there's two major flaws I feel bothered: Lack of lasso fill brushes and flawed selection tools, which I both use very frequently. This is a pretty big dealbreaker for me.

Medibang Paletta

You've heard of Medibang Paint, but Paletta is a more professional sibling of regular Medibang. It has customisable UI and is one-time purchase (currently HK$58 from discount). Aside from that, the functions are almost the same as Medibang Paint...

Except the fact it doesn't have the custom brush function. There isn't a lot of custom brushes for Medibang out there already, so it shouldn't be a big problem for me. However, I often see pretty brushes for Procreate and IbisPaint out there, and man I do feel the FOMO.

Because I am a long-time Medibang user, using Paletta seems like the best option for me. Not only is it relatively cheap, I am also more familiar with the mechanics, no need to worry about relearning everything!

Adobe Fresca

I only learnt about this app today, and it is also free! Hearing the name Adobe, I thought it is some scammy subscription based app, but no, it's free! However, I did not have much high expectations, the two options I have seems good enough, so I felt kinda lazy to even test the app. And HOLY SHIT using the brushes there feels incredibly good! It offers a lot of realistic brushes, it feels as if I'm controlling real pencils and paint brushes.

While that moment I almost immediately decided that this is the one, I immediately encountered one problem, which is the paint bucket tool fills by layer, not canvas. Meaning if I open a new layer under my sketch, and try to paint bucket the area I wanna fill, it'd just paint the entire layer... WHY????? The app also glitched right after I tried to color more stuff, so i didn't really want to use this app anymore. But MAN I HAD HIGH HOPES.

At this point, I am still stuck between Procreate and Paletta, and more leaning towards Paletta. If you guys have any suggestions, please do comment. pls
