Like I said a while ago, I got sick. However, it's a regular amount of sick to me, so I went outside with my relatives. But it seems that my relatives became so concerned about my sneezy nose that they made me visit a clinic, so uhhh, I guess I am very bad at keeping track of my health.

I was a mostly diligent student, unless I got a fever, I always go to school. But now I think of it, I believe there were times that I should've stayed at home. There is one time when I just couldn't stop coughing and sneezing, yet I somehow never thought to leave school early and take a break. There's another where I got weirdly tired after eating lunch, I thought I was just simply tired, but when I checked my temperature after school, it was 40C. There's also the time I got severe food poisoning, but had to leave home cuz I have a test, I didn't even consider I could've just asked my teacher for a retest.

I don't consider myself as hardworking, I'm not the type that goes "Ohhhh I must upload my family's honor and tire myself to death to get good grades", but I'm good at downplaying my pain. From a scale of 1-10, I might be feeling like 5 (which is enough to impact my performance), I would still assume it is fine for me to stay at school. It's kinda like how animals tend to hide their pain to avoid being hunted down, I'm not sure why I keep doing that though.

Anyways, I visited the doctor yesterday, got my medicine and slept for a long time at home, now I'm feeling a bit better. And strangely when I woke up, I had this nostalgic feeling that reminds me of the time during primary school, it's like I transported back in time. Weird.
