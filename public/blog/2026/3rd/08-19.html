Bakatako

It's like ArtFight... but daily

Weather: Hot and randomly rainy

Listening to: あぶく - ヨルシカ / Dongdang (Cover)

Mood: Mixed

Random thought: Cough cough god damn when is my cough gonna go away

Tags: Review, Art
998 words (4 min read)

A while ago, I saw a tweet talking about Bakatako, where artists can share their art, draw people's OCs and draw prompts daily. When people talk about some sort of new and good art website, I often don't feel interested, it's probably gonna die within a few months or at best stay a quiet website. But hearing that it's like a mini ArtFight... I kinda grew interested.

Lately, the art community has gone weary, not only do they need to deal with their own real life stuff, there is also AI sabotaging their art careers/hobbies, drama, and online hostility. Many art posts I see revolve around negativity, it's actually depressing. So recently, I had an idea, to draw random artists' OCs, not because they asked, but I simply surprise them. Receiving art of my OCs is one of the best feelings ever, I want other artists to feel that, even outside of ArtFight. However, the Internet is massive, and I overthink things: What if some artists prefer to not receive fanart? What if they feel stressed about receiving unwarranted gifts? What if the person doesn't receive the art because they aren't active?

Some introductions

Anyways, Bakatako (It means stupid octopus, which is certainly a name) seems to be the perfect platform for me. The main appeal of this website is the daily prompts. You are sorted into one of four teams, then you earn score for your team by drawing the daily prompts. Aside from that, you can also attack people's OCs (I didn't earn any points attacking, but I heard it's because my account has to be 1 week old). While the topics are SFW, Bakatako is meant to be for those 18 or older, so if you are younger, maybe wait a bit longer.

Other ways of earning points is to reply to art, not by text, but by doodles. Not only does the platform encourage interaction between artists, it also pushes us to respond to art with more art, kinda beautiful.

There is also Bakabucks, the currency for the platform which cannot be converted into real money and vice versa. You can commission artists and buy stickers using them.

Experience

Repeating what I said, what I wanted to do the most is to attack people (with art). With the website being new, I don't have to worry about users being inactive. After seeking out some OCs I'm interested in, I drew them on Procreate, then uploaded them. It feels pretty good to see responses from them.

And because I had nothing better to do, I did the daily prompts as well. Not gonna lie, I dislike when I have a topic constraint to my art, it feels like doing homework. It might push me to use my brain more, but man sometimes I just don't find a prompt that fun to draw :(. I did my first prompt using the online drawing program because I did not have my Ipad at that time, but for my second prompt, I drew it on Procreate... Only to realise I have to draw using the online art program for daily prompts unless I am a supporter. Man that's 30 minutes of effort I'm not get back.

On the topic of the online art program, while it has a lot of tools, such as pen pressure and various brushes, one big issue is that if you want to use more advanced brushes, you'd require more GPU. And the problem is, my Ipad certainly doesn't have enough power, because it crashes every time I switch brushes, not even airbrush is safe. So if I have to use that art program, not only I'm not used to it at all, I also have to use the default brush exclusively. So if you check my profile, my daily prompts are often lower quality (It's skill issue but you know, I need time to adapt).

After earning some Bakabucks, which aren't too hard to obtain with the help of daily quests, I often use them to commission artists. I don't really see if Bakabucks are actually useful, you can buy brushes and stickers but I don't really use them, especially not the brushes. However, there are artists willing to earn Bakabucks by drawing other people's OCs, so I'ma try commissioning them. I've received really good art, but I believe many of them underpriced them, so I often tip them, I think most of them need to price their comms 500 Bakabucks or up.

Thoughts

What I love about this platform the most is the interaction between people. In most social media, talking to artists seems intimidating, like what do you tell the artist. In here, the platform encourages people to reply using daily quests, therefore I often see art get replied with compliments written in canvas, or cute/silly doodles related to the art.

Moreover, this platform helps me find OC references easily, I don't need to worry too much, I can just search the tags and find humanoid OCs to draw (I'm focusing on humanoid OCs at the moment).

What I don't enjoy as much has to be the art program thing, while I understand why daily prompts are restricted to avoid spam, I hate how it crashes if I try to use any other brush. Drawing online can be very unstable as well, and I'd lose progress if I lose connection or if I accidentally refresh by scrolling up too low. While there's the supporter perk, I don't like buying subscription services...

Another concern I have is that the site is VERY new, and I'm not certain if the owner can be fully trusted. Yea I did create an account and give them my art and stuff, but hey, nothing can be accoplished if I'm afraid of everything! So far, I don't see anything out of the ordinary, so hope it stays that way!

If you wanna try this platform, you can use my invite here. Now I gotta go to sleep.
