Previously on 4 Sep, I calculated the profits between selling the recipes or their raw ingredients. But after doing all that, I gained the motivation to record the Side Orders, and even the inventory experience of each ingredient/recipe!

Typo corrections

First of all, some of the statistics I written in the previous blog is not correct. I accidentally inputted the Elk price wrong, so it got the prices of the Elk recipes wrong. So, for the most profittable recipes, the first place is Boar Hotpot (217.69%), followed by Grilled Squab (127.54%) and Softshell Hotpot (116.63%). It seems the top three requires a bit of time to finish cooking, no easy way to gain lots of money in a short time! For the least profittable ones, it's Elk Tacos (-24.85%), Ostrich Tartare (-21.71%), and Camel Kebabs (-15.72%). Good thing Elk Tacos (probably) doesn't appear in Side Orders at all, so don't have to worry about selling extras ones in the inventory!

Exp calculations

When you sell something in the inventory, it shows the coin value, but no exp value. So if you want to know how much exp you gained from selling something, you have to calculate it manually from your exp level on the top left corner. While you CAN record the exp of each item, it is very time-consuming and tedicious, nobody with the right mind would want to do it... So it's my turn to do it.

There are 46 ingredients and 100 recipes in Huntcook, selling ingredients is easy, but I would need to wait for recipes to finish cooking. Luckily for me, I've already unlocked all the cooking upgrades, which means it reduces a lot of time to wait for cooking. So I start from the easiest ones like Pheasant Consomme, Tofu, Cider, then slowly move to the recipes that uses other recipes as ingredients... I managed to finish recording them all in two days!

To explain my work process, first I check my current exp level, the level bar might say something like "10687 exp left.", then after I sell the item, I calculate the difference between the two, and divide it by the number of items if I sold stack instead. Rinse and repeat. Most people might question why I would do this tedious task, the answer is because I'm unemployed.

Wiki Editing

I had no experience editing any Fandom Wiki prior to this, I only visited them to learn about character information. After figuring about stuff, I was surprised to find I only need to create an account to modify the wiki contents. After editing some broken links and missing info, I became more motivated to get undocumented info to the wiki.

After finishing calculating the exp, I began editing the wiki pages of each ingredients and recipes, which is a tedicous task again.And sometimes the wiki stops me because I reached rate limit, I wonder if there's a more efficient way to do it... Anyways, I edited all the pages, now it shows the exp value, which might not be very useful to show?

Side Orders

While the exp calculating is annoying, it is a visible finish line. But for side orders? You can go on and on finding new side orders, and you still won't be 100% sure if you found all of them. And one more important thing is some of the side orders only appear in earlier stages, which my current Hunt Cook save file can never get. Therefore, not only I have to note down every new side order I get, I also need to open a new save in my old phone, grind coins, and look for any new side orders. Man it sure is painful.

However, by noting down the side orders, I found out something interesting regarding the rewards. After comparing the numbers, it appears the rewards are calculated from the inventory values of the dishes. For the coin rewards, it is obtained by multiplying the inventory coin values of the dishes by 160%. And for the exp values, it is obtained by multiplying the inventory coin values by 78.4%... I thought it would be by multiplying the inventory exp but it's not related it seems. There's an urge to just automatically let formulas fill in the reward values, but I'll never know if the game decides to break the trend and have an order's rewards that doesn't match my pattern.

While I doubt my side order list is not complete, I decided to upload the list to the Huntcook wiki, perhaps people could help out if they notice it. While I don't know how benefical is my efforts, there's nothing wrong with more archiving, right?
