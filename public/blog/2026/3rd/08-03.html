Damn 47 attacks only? That’s a massive decline compared to last year’s 112 attacks! However, I’m not very mad about the results, in fact I did expect this change (47 attacks is a bit too little tbh).

First of all, I changed medium for attacks, which is by drawing on Ipad. Drawing digitally certainly helped me be more experimental, but it also makes imperfections more obvious, which means I spent more time fixing tiny details which wouldn’t be as noticeable if I were to drew them traditionally. It’s quite fun, but a bit exhausting also.

Second of all, I stopped my profile pic strat (Smaller attacks to achieve more attacks) because this year I wanted to put more effort in each attack. While a lot of artists are happy with any attacks, I wanted to give it my all and perhaps do more character interactions (which I don’t often do).

Third of all, midway through ArtFight, I got very depressed after something happened, and I ended up staying at home doing nothing and skipping meals. I’m better now but yea, unforeseen things happened. But that’s ok, cuz it’s just life.

Despite doing significantly less attacks than last year, the goal I set back in 2025 was 44 attacks, which still meas I passed my average goal!

Four more attacks for them...

Art Fight was ending soon, and I was too tired to look for new people to attack… So I attacked them again.

I only did pencil drawings for these since I had not much time left at that time, but there's something relaxing using pencils only, there's no fuss about cleaniness, colors, details... There's only a pencil and eraser, and I get to just go with the flow~

Not gonna lie, I feel a bit sad about not doing attacks as much. Since the amount of attacks I do are proportional to the defenses I have, I kinda wished that people can draw my OCs more (>- The greed they talk about in the bible). Anyways, I really love the art I am given this year and previous seasons, it's always special seeing people put thought into drawing them. I especially love the ones that includes character interactions and goofy stuff, like Storyteller summoning hampter Cosmos out of her pocket and The RS gang giving Jaspers outfit ideas (And Jaspers wearing a maid outfit), jesus christ I was going crazy by just seeing the thumbnails.

Anyways, I might do a celebratory art for this year's AF, like always. Thank you for the people who worked hard this month, you guys deserve some rest <3
