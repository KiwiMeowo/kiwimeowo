So recently, me and my sister watched the Chiikawa movie, The Secret of the Mermaid Island. While I believe Chiikawa isn't very popular in the West, it is quite well known here. And I frequently see Chiikawa clips in my timeline, so I managed to know a fair amount of lore by osmosis. Spoilers for The Secret of the Mermaid Island

Premise and the intro

If you don't know much about Chiikawa, I don't think you need a very deep knowledge in order to understand the movie. It is a series featuring animal-like characters going through their daily life, they can be enjoying their lives being with friends and eating tasty food, or they can also face some very tough problems which people may find relatable or depressing. The main trio are Chiikawa (The timid hamster-like protagonist), Hachiware (The kind friend who is the only one who can talk within the trio) and Usagi (The eccentric friend who is strangely capable).

The story of this film is that the trio finds a susprious poster that advertises a really high paying job which offers a fun trip to an island and tasty food, which quickly attracted their interest... It sounds a lot like those jobs that sounds too good to be true and kidnaps people who didn't know better. Once they arrived at the island, they are welcomed by the islanders, who offered them entertainment, food and a place to rest.

All is well until they found a cave, which is the home to the Siren and two mermaids- Well there used to be one more mermaid, but it got eaten, and Siren is seeking for the person who is responsible for it. The trio then encountered another problem, which is that the islanders wants them to defeat the Siren, since she is kidnapping (and eating) islanders to look for the murderer. So now, they have to look for ways to resolve the case one way or the other.

To battle the Siren

At first, the trio and Rakko (Their mentor that looks serious but loves sweets) tried to negotiate with the Siren, but the multiple times they did failed, especially after what Momonga (An annoying "cute flying squirrel") did. Seriously Momonga, it keeps sabotaging the teams plans. They eventually learnt that the motive of eating the mermaid might be due to their flesh being able to give one eternal life, and the Siren said when she finds the culprit, she will trap them in a cage under the sea for eternity...

Also when the team got captured by Siren, they tried to struggle themselves out of the kelps Siren tied them with. When they managed to break the kelp half way through, Siren just stared at ehm, like a teacher staring at rowdy kids sitting at the back with a creepy smile. It was hilarious.

Not the movie screenshot but you get the idea

After getting out, they realised just talking to Siren isn't enough, so they decided to neutralise her singing powers by feeding her extra spicy curry. Chiikawa asked Ichiya and Futaba (The two islanders who have been taking care of the trio) to look for ingredients, but when they are searching in the kitchen drawer, a piece of mermaid scale flew out, which Chiikawa decided to keep in its pocket.

After a long battle, they managed to feed Siren the curry, and she succumbed onto the ground while rolling in pain. The islanders all gathered around her, yelling to give her the final blow. While Siren did kill many islanders, but Chiikawa and the others felt it isn't right to just kill her. And seeing her screaming in pain is actually disturbing, it's as if the voice actor is being tortured.

While Siren was rolling on the ground, she whacks her tail towards the trio, but Futaba immediately pushes them away, directly facing the attack. Siren then says "I get it! I get it now! I won't attack the islanders any longer!" then runs away. The team and Ichiya moves Futaba into their room, and while they are trying to find ways to treat Futaba's injuries, a major secret is revealed: Ichiya opens up a battery slot, and inserts a battery inside Futaba. Immediately, Futaba wakes up, and unfortunately, Chiikawa witnessed all of it.

The dark secret

When Siren and the three mermaids arrived at the island, while the islanders were afraid of them, they are still willing to serve them because their singing powers help their crops grow. They have a pretty good relationship until one day, Futaba and Ichiya were fishing while Siren and the mermaids were playing nearby. Ichiya was somewhat fearful of Siren, Futaba comforted that they are helpful for the islands. Suddenly, Siren accidentally knocks over their boat, and gravely injured Futaba.

Seeing Futaba in this horrible state, Ichiya was furious at Siren, and which they remembered the property of mermaid flesh: Eternal life. In order to save its friend's life, they snuck into Siren's cave, and murdered one of the mermaids in cold blood, the whacks that attacked the mermaid felt uncomfortably realistic... After feeding Futaba the mermaid meat, Futaba was terrified: If it lives forever, one day everyone around it will die, including Ichiya. So Futaba forced Ichya to eat the mermaid flesh, and after that, they pinky promised to never tell anyone else about this incident.

On the night after the battle, Chiikawa approaches Ichiya and Futaba, intending to confront them regarding the truth. But eventually, it decides not to, perhaps telling the whole truth won't do any good at this point... Poor Chiikawa, having to bear the weight of not telling the truth, having to hide the fact two people it knew committed murder... And during this scene, a song that played near the beginning plays again, one of the lyrics is "Let's be friends forever", which in this context, is a bit scary.

On the day of departure, Chiikawa finally shows the mermaid scale to its friends. But it eventually lets go of the scale, letting it fly back into the ocean. I didn't think of it much, but it might have meant that "What happened in the island, stays in the island".

Oh god...

Back to the POV of Ichiya and Futaba, they were walking into the forest discussing the time when Futaba got knocked over by Siren's tail. The reason Futaba collapse is because their battery got knocked off, and Siren witnessed that. So when Siren said "I get it! I will stop attacking the islanders", it wasn't because the islanders scared her off, but she found the culprits.

In the credits scene, the two rowed their boat to a new island to avoid Siren's pursuits. They found a cave as a new home, and perhaps they can start a new life! The camera slowly pans out, showing the island's view and the ocean around... Then Siren and her mermaid appears, swimming towards the island. The end...

Thoughts

Ok, I was already aware of how dark Chiikawa's stories can be prior to watching this movie. I actually heard of some plot points regarding this movie, I only knew about potential cannibalism (It isn't really cannibalism) and some people changed their dinner from yakiniku to ramen, but the whole build up of horror is really good. When the movie was nearing its end, I wasn't particularly disturbed, just interested by the whole morally grey plot going on. But when the post-credits scene came, the horror hit like a train, those poor little guys have to suffer for eternity now.

And for the morally grey part, there isn't really anyone that is fully right or wrong. For Ichiya, it kills one of the mermaids to save its friend after being attacked by Siren (accidentally), giving Futaba eternal life (which is more of a curse than blessing). Futaba, after being fed the mermaid flesh, forces Ichiya to eat it as well in fear of being alone. As justified its anger is, putting its friend in the curse is ultimately a selfish act. And because the mermaid was killed, Siren's trust towards the islanders is broken. And for Siren, while it is reasonable to be furious, she had sacrificed many innocent lives searching for the murderer. And not only that, the punishment she gave the culprit is to drown them under the sea for eternity, a fate worse than death.

This thread mentions what both parties did wrong, which is the lack of communication. Siren is capable of communication, so when Futaba got injured, Ichiya could've negotiated with Siren, so a small piece of the mermaid's flesh can be extracted to save Futaba's life without causing the mermaid too much harm. But because of Ichiya's prior prejudice towards Siren and the mermaids, it killed the mermaid without hesitation. And when Siren discovered the murder, she could've told the islanders about this, and perhaps look for the murderer together. But she didn't, instead she started attacking all the islanders without explaination.

Dark stuff aside, I really love how much details and foreshadowing there is. For example, when Hachiware asks Ichiya and Futaba for carbonated drinks, the two got confused for a moment, it was because "Carbonated drinks" and "Battery" sound the same in Japanese. Also, Siren's sleeping pose changes in different time periods. Before one of the mermaid got killed, she sleeps belly side up, but after the incident, she sleeps side ways, allowing herself to be more alert... Ok this is still dark, but still a fun fact.

Overall, this is a really good film from start to end, even those who didn't watch Chiikawa will enjoy (and get traumatised by) the film!
