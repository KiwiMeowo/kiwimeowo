Procreate Review

After 2 months of using

Weather: weather

Listening to: Yaelokre - Neath the grove is a heart

Mood: Anxious

Random thought: Sometimes I would think I'm a potential criminal

Tags: Discussion
669 words (3 min read)

It's been a while since I switched from Medibang to Procreate, and to explain it in a few words: It's really great. So here's a list of thoughts I have about this app, and it's not totally because I need to prepare my website for a new quarter blog page.

Things I enjoyed

Lot's of good brushes

First of all, the are plenty of brushes to use. I didn't really need to tune the brushes, I just grabbed the HB Pencil brush and it was the perfect line art brush for me. There's so many brushes that I felt kinda overwhelmed, I also experiemented with a lot of coloring brushes.

Centurion feels like my real life alcohol brushes at first, but I felt kinda picky with it. Even after tuning the settings in many ways, I still didn't feel satisfied. I do feel kinda bad, because it feels like it was trying to fulfill my impossible request to be both realistic and easy to control, kinda like this video.

There's also the Penguin and Tidewalker watercolor brushes, which I enjoy using them for simpler drawings and blending. However, I prefer not using them for my main drawings, because they are lighter in color, like watercolors do! And I prefer my drawings to match the way the color wheel shows.

And recently I started using Miena, a pastel brush. It's quite solid, but there's also unpainted spots, which feels kinda realistic! I'm gonna try it a bit more to have a full opinion, but so far I enjoy it!

Pretty good UI

In Medibang, I only got to draw without the ads because I turned off Wi-Fi, even then the area above the canvas reserved for ads doesn't go away. Therefore, the drawing area is quite cramped. In Procreate, it feels so much cleaner, no disturbances, big drawing area...

Did I mention good brushes?

Looking back at Medibang, the pencils and blending brushes felt primative. You can't set the pressure levels of the brushes, and it felt very artifical. And because not a lot of people use Medibang, there isn't a lot of custom brushes out there. Yes I could work with them, but they are less fun.

In Procreate, there's a lot of custom brushes made by other people, and some of them are free! And drawing with Procreate brushes, it feels amazing in a way I couldn't describe.

Now, here's the cons

Crappy selection tool

It lacks the close gap option in the fill tool. If you draw the line art, and one of the lines didn't fully close, the colors will leak out of the area upon using the fill tool.

It lacks doesn't have the expand area option, which is insanely useful for adding borders. Sure you can use the feather tool to work your way around it, but it takes so much more steps, it's annoying.

Lack of lasso fill

Using Procreate for 2 months, I kinda lost the need to use the lasso tool. Still, it'd be great for them to add it.

And yea, theat's the complaints I have at the momemt. I've look up in Google, people had asked for these changes years ago, but Procreate never really implemented them. While I'm somewhat pessimistic about them being added, they actually implemented colorpicking on active layer around 4 months ago, so I guess I don't need to give up in hope yet (I actually discovered this function accidentally, I nudges the screen while colorpicking and toggled the function on).

Despite the flaws and all, it didn't stop me from drawing on my Ipad all the time now. It doesn't give me the disconnected feeling while drawing with my laptop and tablet, and doesn't consume my storage space like physical drawings. And it is pretty portable: If I were to bring my Ipad to commute, I can draw with it when I sit down (I just need to be lucky enough to find a seat...). Man I should've switched from tablet to Ipad sooner.
