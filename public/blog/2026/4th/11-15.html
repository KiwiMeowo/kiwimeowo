Me and Fan-Fiction, and the Outage of AO3

1033 words (4 min read)

Despite my love for consuming my favourite media's fan content, I was not much of a fan fic reader. I've seen the passion of fic readers and writers, especially on AO3, but I somehow didn't really understand the hype. However, with the 2+ weeks downfall of AO3, I did start to notice how important it is.

I actually tried to read fics back in my Danganronpa fixation era, I don't think I was looking for anything much particular, probably just anything related to Kirumi. Sadly, I couldn't anything much interesting back then, and many of the fics I've read were kinda out of character, so they didn't catch get my heart.

Another thing is might have been the reason why I didn't read much fics is because I don't ship a lot of characters. From what I've seen from what people say, AO3 is very popular when it comes to ship fics, there's of course ones with non-romance stuff, but when I hear about fics, it's always ship stuff.

The another more minor reason is that I don't like reading, which I doubt contributed a lot, because when it comes to my fav medias, I would consume all related material.

But over the years, I did read fics that caught my eye.

Eyes on the Ball! By Kungukingi

It's a Blue Lock fic, featuring Barou x Isagi, which is a ship I do like. I think I found it after a BaroSagi fan recommended it on Twitter. It was a pretty good fic, Barou gets an injury worse than Chigiri's tho so that's still sad.

I also really love one of the quotes, so much so that I saved in my notes:

“So just so you’ll know — I’ll watch all of it, savour every last image. And I won’t fucking stop until I go blind from having stared at the sun for too long.”

I love me some gay rivalry.

The Case of The Missing Soulstones By Mf99k

I read this fic because, well, my friend wrote the fic. I think she told us to read the fic in Discord, then I just picked it up and spent the commuting time reading the whole thing. It's about Almond solving the case of stolen Soulstones, and Walnut solving the case of missing children, and also there's a new cookie named Caprese. There's death, betrayal, and time shenanigans, of course!

Moreover, this fic is tied in a series, after reading this one, you can go to read the later ones, where Caprese gets into more unfortunate situations and gets eaten yet again. The series is very fun to read, and because the TBD is involved, you just know how chaotic it can get. Even after I quit Cookie Run, I still continued following the series.

Discordant by EvieNyx

I found this fic very recently, and if you have saw my pic feed, you will know I had shared it. One day while watching Coley's video about BloodyMary (The ship between Ryland Grace and Simon from Iron Lung, yup it's a crackship), and I was like "What kind of PHM fics does AO3 have?", then I went and found this fic. And I must say, DAMN it's the best fic I've read, even though there's not much competition.

This fic is about Ryland time travelling back to the point before Sharpio and Dubois got killed in the lab accident, and everyone around him got very confused. And in this fic, we got to know about the humans a bit more, even the regular ones outside of the project. I really love how we get glimpses of the social media under the threat of Astrophages, like people making memes, people being dumb, the fears and hopes. There's also the author's interpretationss of Eridian culture and religion, while we would never know the canon world building for Eridian religion, and new addition in the fic is actually really interesting to read, and quite beautiful at times!

I don't think my words do that fic justice, because the fic actually made me so damn emotional, like if you watched PHM already, please read the fic.

Alright review round is over! I finally have the excuse to recommend the fics I enjoyed, I wasn't sure if I should start a new page in my site for the fics because there would be only 3 entries. Now let's talk about the Great Outage of AO3, which occured on 30th October.

I haven't really touch social media lately, so I have no idea how badly the AO3 frequenters took the news. As for me, I wasn't so disturbed at the time, well it's only 2-4 weeks, so it couldn't be that bad. Moreover, it's not like I read fics much, so waiting for fics isn't an iss- wait.

DISCORDANT IS STILL ONGOING

The newest update at the time was 11 Sep 2026, so I doubt it would update too quickly. However, what I was more concerned about was if AO3 would somehow be down for an even longer time, or even an indefinite time... That's the worst case scenario. I went to Wayback Machine (Thank god for wayback machine), and the fic did get archived, but it didn't have the newest chapter, so I was slightly scared.

Somehow during the outage, I started having new thoughts about fics. I would be like "Oh I see a lot of people are dissatisfied by the TADC ending because of these reasons, which I agree, I wonder if someone made a fix-it fic exploring the characters more?" or "Maybe I should archive these fics in case if the author deletes them...", which is all I couldn't do because AO3 is down.

Luckily, today, AO3 went back up. I don't have a good reason to open Twitter right now, but I bet everyone was freaking out. I just now downloaded the fics I want to archive, and maybe I will look up some fics I'm curious about.
