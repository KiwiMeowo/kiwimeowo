Yesterday, I completed the prologue for Upon the Starry Sky, it came out way better than I ever hoped it to be!.. Well of course it isn't perfect by all means, but it's better to be completed than be forever in my imagination, right?

Retconning

Looking back some of the blogs and OC lore I've written, I realised some of them had changed or outdated. One big change is the rumor about a dangerous creature in an artifact, I wasn't very sure if I should make Cosmos a presumed threat to humans, but eventually I decided that there's no dangerous creature people were afraid of. However, days prior to the prologue, there was a beam of energy shooting up into the sky, which caught people's attention, including Lavender.

For some stories I've written, like Rainbow Hortensia and Lavender's background mentioned in my blogs, while they're still applicable to the canon lore, I might modify some details a bit, because Cosmos knew a bit more about Lavender. Mira's personality is also a little bit different, she knew a lot way before she met Lavender.

The history on how I wrote UtSS (really slowly)

Let's start with the beginning on how the story of UtSS changed. The story concept is based off my childhood daydreams, where I became a magical girl, went to a magical world hidden inside the bedside table drawer, and beat up bad guys. It is what people may call a paracosm, I used to daydream about it during primary school, and even back in kindergarten! But as I grew up and became more depressed, I spent less time thinking about this world, at some point I imagined this world self-destructing.

After the dream I met Lavender and Mira, it felt like a divine message to pursue their story more. I was then reminded of my paracosm, I was like "Hey why not merge them together?". The story that was created is Mira landing in a magical, barren world, and meeting Yume, starting a journey to return to Mira's world... Except Mira doesn't want to (I mentioned this before). Eventually I hit a dead end and forgot about this story again.

Fast forward to the day I created Cosmos, at first I had no idea what story I want Cosmos to have. For a while, she was stuck alone in my head, all alone and sad. I then developed an idea about a world full of kemonomimis with wings. They are created by accident by an angel, causing animals on the world to mutate to have angel features. Cosmos, Jaspers, Bun, Penelope and an unnamed OC with a bird helmet was in a team, where they fight back the angel, who wanted to fix the mistake by destroying that world. Cosmos was also an anti-villain, who would betray others to save her world. Jaspers was a villain I think, but I forgot in what way.

I later wanted to merge Lavender's story and Cosmos' together, Lavender befriends Wolf and Raven (who both was in my childhood paracosm) and travels to Cosmos' world together in the second act. However, I realised the process is a little convoluted, so I ditched the angel world concept entirely.

The structure of the lore starts to form

And while Lavender was the protagonist, I had a clear favoritism towards Cosmos, therefore I spent more time thinking about Cosmos' backstory. The old concept is that there are quite a few Cometlings roaming around, the scientists captured a few, and used their powers on probably somthing related to saving the world. It was also when Cosmos met her human friend, which won't survive for long. Reading my old notes, it seemed Cosmos used to be more antagonistic, who absorb Lavender's power to revert to her stronger form, only stopped because Lavender knew offensive skills and the fact Lavender has no magic essence inside her... I guess this is where I got the part of "Cosmos was known to be a dangerous creature hidden in the woods".

For Lavender, she is a wizard who failed to fit in with others, and lost her magic powers after trying too hard to use magic. She hid away from society since the lack of magic essence is like being dead (I even thought about making Lavender to be black and white, but her purple colors were too iconic), up until she met Cosmos, who tried to attack her. Lavender kept Cosmos in a cage, but she suddenly got in danger (monsters attacked them probably), that's when they decided to work together. Since Lavender wants magic, and Cosmos need a host to earn energy, they... go to school.

Honorable mention, there was a character who's Lavender's roommate, who is based off a character in my childhood paracosm. In my childhood story, after the protgonist (me) loses her wand and magical familiar, a girl comforts her with a rare flower. And that's what this character is based off, and her purpose in the story is to be Lavender's human friend. But gentle healer is such an overdone trope, how about a healer who prefers to kick ass, don't you agree, Wolf?

Also hey unused concept of Cosmos taking over Lavender's body! It feels a little cursed to see them like that...

Holy FUCK she's back

The main story of UtSS is that the world is dying, so I figured the next characters I make will be a hero who ventures into dangerous areas (now known as the Voided Lands) but sadly went missing. And you guessed it, it's the RS team's turn!

Perhaps the first appearance of Grey and Claire after many years.

To expand the team, I merged the scientist team mentioned above, the remaining angels team, and added Claire to the mix. A team of brave heroes, travelling into certain death... Then I noticed that a hail mary like that is not necessary at all, so I instead made them to be idiots who somehow thought going there is worth risking.

Mira, and the concept of Hero/Villain

The above storywriting I've mentioned are what I've written in my notes, but for the rest, a lot of only stored in my memories, so there might be some gaps. While I have found notes about Mira betraying Lavender, even past me said I didn't like this idea. But I had an idea: What if they were forced to fight each other. Lavender and Mira might have genuinely liked each other, but perhaps fate didn't allow them, maybe the god themselves had to make them battle to death for the greater good? Yayy conflict...

Then I thought in Lavender's position, being isolated by others, finally finding someone who genuinely cares about you, only to find out only one of you can live, then be responsible for your friend's death? Man if that were me, I would handle living past that... Oh god Lavender might try to off herself-

And that's how Lavender (and technically Mira) became partially immortal, all because of the somewhat morbid thinking. But also because of that, some more worldbuilding got extended, such as them being the Hero/Villain and it being a literal plot armor. While it feels a little lame to say this, but the lore got more and more meta afterwards.

What does it mean to be the Hero? What does it mean to be the Villain? Maybe some day I will find the opportunity to explain it. I hope my story isn't too ass, and maybe those who liked my characters will love to see more about Cosmos, Lavender and Mira!
