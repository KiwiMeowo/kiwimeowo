I have reviewed this game before in the blog of 25 Aug 2024, but it was very brief, and the game was still in beta. The full version got released like a year ago, but I didn't buy it immediately;

  • I don't want to play this on an emulator or my house desktop computer
  • I wanted to check more reviews

After reading the reviews, there's mainly these complaints:

  • Poor translations
  • Gameplay is lackluster
  • The fics feel AI-generated (Unproven)

Thanks to the fact I speak Chinese, I can perfectly understand the game in its original language, perks of being bilingual uwo~🟊.That means only two flaws left, so I still needed some convincing to play it. Well, if Steam has a 30% discount on the game, I will actually try it, I said few days ago...

Well, guess what happened?

Premise

We play as a fan girl, default Internet name SqueamySquid, default real life name Mila, who ships a ship very religiously, and saves her fics only to her hard drive. But one day, she accidentally left her drive at school, and very unfortunately, Cordelia picked up and read it. Luckily, she encourages Mila to share the fics to the Internet. And so here goes our fanfic journey~

If you have been following me for a certain time, you may be like "Hmmm, Kiwi often draws Lavender x Mira, she would definitely put them in the game, right?"

WRONG >:3

I'm not very picky in terms of ships, also I didn't want to make a new character into the game, it was very difficult to manage them. Also back when I first saw the background image, I squealed like a fan girl that I was. Imagine my OCs... My ships... Showing up on the billboards. Man I would be quaking with excitement every time I'm outside.

The main focus of the game is writing fics, but of course, the game doesn't expect you to write a full fic like a teacher giving you homework. Instead, it would print out a pre-written story based off the title and the tags, and sometimes it would let you choose the routes. Then everytime you post, you earn fans. And if you do a commission, you earn money!

A little warning here: Some tags may contain tags that cause discomfort in some people, like noncon. However, for sexual topics, it's either a fakeout (A forced B into the room, and watched Naruto together), or it's only lightly implied. Moreover, there's an option to filter the tags you dislike, but I didn't use the button nor I checked where to find it (Honestly I should've used it, some fics, like the race car one, did make me uncomfortable.).

Not gonna translate the whole thing, but the summary is Lavender's a vampire, Cosmos's a werewolf who moved in near her house and started singing Never Gonna Give You Up, so they fought

But to write fics, you must get inspirations. By visiting various places (School, theatre, bookstore...), you can brainstorm prompts and fic ideas. Aside from that, you can also earn money or some other stats that I don't really care about.

"During group discussion, I confidently opened my laptop in front of my groupmates, which the screen shows the half-finished LavCosmos fic I wrote last night."
"Got called to answer a question, and confidently answered the title of the fic I'm secretly reading"

Another pretty cool thing, it teaches you a lot about fanculture. In this game, you can decorate your room and itabag with your fav ships' merch. You can sell you zines containing the fics you wrote, and sometimes the story will explain what the fandom terms meant!

Now I know what the Chinese word for callout posts is!

Speaking of characters, there are 7 characters in the game you interact with:

  • RippleBoat (Cordelia): Our classmate who discovered out hard drive fics, and encourages us to go fangirl mode. Her smiles always feels so menacing.
  • VelvetYummy (Oralie): The fandom celebrity, holds a lot of fandom events.
  • EtherealSnow (Blanche): The fic writer who is also employed (unrelated to fic writing), looks suspiciously like the goddess who appears in our dreams.
  • WraithLantern (Nora): The shipper of another ship involving one of the characters in your ship (called 對家), but somehow keeps reading your fics.
  • There's also our grandma (NightLastsNever), ScarletFlames and CobaltWaters, but I didn't visited their interactions much yet.

While I wasn't particularly interested in interacting with them at first, I did grew to enjoy their characters, especially during times they discuss toxic fandom stuff. The major example here is Nora, she and her other like-minded shippers attacked the protag's fics, saying how bad it is. The characters also discussed the consequences of overly strict fandom rules and how some people would resort to deleting their accounts after being cyberbullied. I do wish the game would go in the Internet aspect of fandoms more, but it would go toxic fast lmao.

The context is the protag removed her lipstick because she's too nervous to wear makeup in front of Blanche. Afterwards Blache noticed a smudge of lipstick on her lips, but before the protag explained the situation, Blanche imagined the scenario of a character in the ship meeting someone while their lips has the lipstain from someone else... Then the protag agrees with a straight face. Classic fangirl behavior.

My favorite character is perhaps Nora, despite being a bully in the fandom. If you want to avoid spoilers: She usually do things not because she liked them, but because other people liked her doing it (Like she draws to please her mother, she also takes care of cats and elderly because people like her to be charitable). While she was enjoying a ship, she stumbled upon our fics on the ship opposing hers, and she couldn't handle her feelings: It was really good, but she can't like it because she ships the other. And also because the group she was in was toxic, her choice was to harass the opposing shippers.

But by fate, the protag met Nora because Nora is the caretaker of the protag's grandma. They slowly started to chat about fandom stuff and the woes of shipping. Eventually, they both realised their identities on the Internet. Instead of being mad at the protag, Nora decided to apologise for everything she did to her and the shippers, and also realises her newfound love for our ship.

But what I love the most about Nora is the way she interacts with others. While she was quite mean being a bully online, she's actually quite insecure. After becoming friends with the protag, she clings onto her a lot and avoided interacting with the other fangirls, in fear of them still hating her for what she did. And it did take a lot of time for her to warm up with the others, but eventually she did. Her character feels realistic, not saying others aren't but the main storyline explains her character the most.

While the writings and characters are a important factor, I do really like the picutres of the little Cosmos and Lavender. While they just use the dressup puppets we made at the beginning, I'm a visual person and enjoys my OCs wearing different stuff.

Some complaints

After playing the game, I understand the negative reviews on the Steam pages a bit more. Firstly, the game feels repetitive really quickly. The gameplay loop is usually go to a place, gain ideas, write them out, sleep, then loop. After I reached mid game, I focused on finding fics with relevant tags so that I can unlock some zines, but it does need a bit RNG to get the one I want.

Perhaps because I have gotten more idea on how my OCs interact compared to 2024, the OOC moments in the fics feels greater, and I just couldn't really connect with the characters that seems like they only share the same name as my OCs. Well not like the game can read our brains and convey the characters the way we wanted, either way, the quality of the fics isn't that good, I liked the funny ones though.

Another complaint I have is the mechanics of the game didn't tie very well together. There is a Tamogotchi to take care of your ship, and minigames inside to earn levels, but I don't really see any reason to play with it, because I was focused on the fic unlocking. TBH they could've combined the desktop pet, where the characters doesn't interact at all, with the Tamagotchi function, so that I can watch my blorbos do their thing without needing to pick up the Tamagotchi.

There's also a lot of merch you can collect, but idk if it was me, but I felt a little aimless with them. Sure it'd be nice to decorate my room and itabag with merch of my OCs, but I wouldn't say most of them look particularly aesthetic, and I still need to decorate to gain points to unlock more stuff. Therefore, I just stuff my room with as much products as possible, and it doesn't look really good. Also I don't find the need to own more than one itabag, but since I need to get more points, so yea.

I do want a bag sole for my OC stuff, but it usually means heavier bags when going out... And I don't have enough OC products in the first place...

It also gets buggy sometimes, like how Cosmos in the Tamagotchi zooms up in the screen, and I couldn't see Lavender anymore. And also when I was making a freebie badge, I clicked something and it softlocked my game, losing 2 hours of progress.

Now, this is more of a suggestion, but I do wish one game can incorporate multiple characters, or oven ships. Nora's existence in the game implies there are other ships that may be popular. While it might make the ship look bad, and while many fangirls strictly feed on one singular ship content, I do want to add my other ships as Nora's ship. Or maybe have other characters inserted to appear in backgrounds, I mean you can add other desktop characters aside from your ship, so why not merch as well?

And that's all folks! For this game, there's a lot of potential since there's not a lot of games revolving fandom culture, but the gameplay is somewhat lacking. I'd rate it 5/10, I do hope there will be more updates, but it's been a year, and there isn't much news...
