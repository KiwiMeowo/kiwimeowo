Back in 2024 , I made two plushies, one for Cosmos and one for Lavender. However, I don't feel that satisfied doing only these two plushies: I wanted to make 10cm ones.

As much as I love my plushies, there are two things I felt incomplete. One, they are my first attempts at making plushies, therefore they look quite off. It might be because I didn't lock the embroidery hoop tight enough, their facial features shrunk quite a lot, with Lavender being the most obvious, as her eyes is constantly squinting. And two, it is a bit inconvenient to bring 20cm sized plushies, they occupy a somewhat big portion in my bag, and I don't have a bag that can both properly show my plushies and also store my belongings safely.

So my decision was to make new 10cm plushies, which I can get a good looking itabag to put them in and show the world how much I love my characters who only exists in my brain. (It would take me a while to consider what kind of Itabag I want to make though).

From this page where I got the 20cm plushie template from, it also contains the 10cm version. However, I didn't realise that, and simply searched for 10cm Nui plushie template instead. My result is this template by Tacute, which website also contains other resources and templates for plushies, like hair templates and clothing templates, really helpful!

Because I had made some plushies before, I have a small idea on how the parts are attached, so I went to work quite quickly. I did Lavender first this time, because I ran out of fabric for Cosmos. I made the eyes bigger and screwed the embroidery hoop tighter (I even used a wrench!), and instead of putting the hair under the embroidery sheet when embroidering the hair and face together, I just put it above the embroidery sheet for easier control (Later I realised I need to add another layer of embroidery sheet above, even if I know where the lines go, the fur on the fabric will pass through the threads without the embroidery sheet). The entire time I was praying the embroidery won't be warped, and I kept checking if the hoop is tight enough.

The results, she's so tinyyyyy

It turned out so much better compared to making the 20cm ones! Arghhhh she's so cute I must squish!!!!!!! One interesting feature is that the head and the body aren't attached before you stuff them, which means they are attached using ladder stitches. While it is doable, Lavender head feels very floppy, which also combines the fact her head is massive compared to her body.

Head banging

Another great thing is that in Tacute's website, there's also a hair braid pattern, which helped me do Lavender's hair, I don't need to meticulously cut of fabric strands, braid them and attached them to the head apparently.

The process is mostly similar for Cosmos, embroider the face, embroider the hair on the face, stitch the head together, make the body, combine them and done!

Girl why are you so ears

The results are quite satisfactory, no embroidery warping, the hair shape is as intended, overall there isn't much mess making them.

Oh I forgot to mention, there's another reason why I want to make 10cm plushies. You see, back in June, I went to Osaka with my boyfriend, and stumbled upon a gacha machine with 10cm plushie clothing, I was quite stoked seeing them. But of course, they don't fit my 20cm Cosmos plushie, and even after getting one to test if there's any miracle that it fits, it didn't.

So now that I have a 10cm Cosmos plushie, let's see how it looks...

Amazing

Her body is slightly smaller than the outfit intended, so I simply tightened the straps a bit more, downsizing is easier than upsizing.

Another thing, I also made animal overalls for Lavender using Tacute's template!I have a lot of spare fabric lying around, don't want to waste them!
