Man after reaching adulthood, it feels as if everytime I get sick or has any health problems, it gets very stubborn. I mean sure I don't get colds as much compared to when I was a kid, but now that I have to visit doctors on my own, makes me worry about money a lot.

I won't say I get sick very easily when I was a kid, but I do remember the many times I get coughs and had to spend a week eating medicine. While it was nice of doctors to gift sick children candy, I really, REALLY hate liquid medicine. Even if adults try to make the medicine look appetising, like calling the brown colored liquid "cola", or the pink, thick solution "strawberry milk", it doesn't remove the fact it tastes horribly gross. The only liquid medicine I could bear was the cough medicine, everything else makes me wanna throw up, or just makes me throw up.

I did not pick pills at the time because I feared that I couldn't swallow pills and choke on the spot, but I realised some of the pills are really small, and I can actually swallow bigger pills than I thought. When I switched to pills when I get sick, I began to fear taking medicine a bit less.

After growing up, getting colds and fevers becomes less frequent. But in turn, I get weird conditions on my body, like random itches, ear infections, eye pain... And at the times I get colds, I might have headaches and sinus pain, which didn't happen when I was young! Maybe this is what happens in adulthood, or maybe this is some post-covid shit.

To be fair, I don't exactly take care of my body a lot. I don't exercise much, I forget to eat my veggies, I sit like a shrimp (Hey you, yes you, sit up straight now). But it is pretty annoying to see any annoyances in my body might take a long time to heal, as opposed to three days. I especially hate when I finally got through a busy week, only to be sick and unable to do the things I enjoy. I wanted to spend the whole day drawing today, but I feel very sick so I have to endure my headache while doodling.
