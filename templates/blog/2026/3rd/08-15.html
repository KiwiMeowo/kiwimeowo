When I was younger, I had a friend who also draws. We used to share our drawings and talk about our interests, but unfortunately, we fell out, and I stopped having contact with her. While I still have bitter feelings, I miss the times when I had a friend that always listened when I share my OCs.

In my earlier days on the Internet, I find friends through my interests, like Minecraft, Sky COTL, Cookie Run. Because of the shared interests, there is people who I would spend a long time chatting with. However, it really all comes down to luck whether I find friends. Internet is full of people, and unless you stand out or knows how to manipulate the algorithm, you get easily forgotten.

While I did find artist friends, the server is mostly quiet, or I couldn't find a common interest to chat with. Sometimes I will search online for places to find artist friends, but I get so overwhelmed I couldn't start. And even if I find some people to follow, it is difficult to keep up with a conversation, and eventually we drift apart.

One issue I have is that back when I used social media to post my art, 99% of the time it barely gets seen. There are even times my art gets 0 likes, I know algorithm can just do that, but it feels as if nothing matters. I know there is a random chance my post will get blessed by the algorithm, but seeing again and again that my posts get unseen feels like being told "Hey, nobody cares about your art".

So yea, it is hard to make artist friends, and I'm bad at maintaining friends as well. But I do want something to change, especially how toxic art community can be and the whole genAI thing. So many artists feel discouraged from sharing their art, and feel so disconnected because socmed encourages competitiveness more than community. I really want things to change, even if I don't know how.
