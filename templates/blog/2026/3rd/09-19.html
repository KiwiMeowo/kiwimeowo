In this blog of 1 Jan 2025, I mentioned collecting stickers and stuff, which includes dress up stickers. Thinking back, I wondered why I hadn't elaborated on that topic, because I loved playing dress up games or character creators as a kid. Talking abt these two types here because they are so similar in nature.

Being a kid with little internet restrictions, I played a lot of flash games. One of the flash games I remember playing was those flashy (pun not intended) dress up games. There was one dress up game with Hello Kitty, one Hello Kitty song was playing while the background glittered, it was surreal.

When I was a younger artist, I did not have money for commissions, energy for art trades, or the attention to be gifted OC art. So how did I experience the joy of my OCs in pretty artstyles? Well I went on character creator websites/apps. The website people probably know the most might be Picrew, but there are other pretty good websites, including Neko and Charat. There's also Kisakae (which doesn't have a browser version), and I remember there's a Japanese dress up game which has endless wardrobe, so much that I went dizzy.

One unfortunate thing about dress up games is that no matter how many items the artists add, there will be a specific edge case where a user couldn't add an accessory because none of the items remotely look like it, or the color combinations could match what they want. This happened to me a lot when I make Cosmos in a character creator, I wouldn't be able to add both her tail and wings, and making her wing colors wouldn't be possible at all. Moreover, these websites usualyy don't have a wide range of body types, so it's kinda hard to make Cosmos accurate, since her body is more muscular.
