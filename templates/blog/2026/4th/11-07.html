Remember the blog about kayaking? I did mention that I would want to take care in more nature cleaning events, so this year, I have been joing more!

Like I said before, I have a mild passion of nature cleaning, therefore I always wanted to join these events. And with my extra time of not being employed, I decided to partake in them through Nature Rescue. However, the path to joing these events can be rather rocky at first... I kept being unable to join them from various unpredicatable reasons. The event either gets cancelled due to bad weather, or that I would suddenly have special events to take part in. It happened like 4 consecutive times until I actually successfully joined one, I guess taking part in one during summer would be difficult.

I joined multiple beach cleaning events, and the general gist of the events are the same: The leader would introduce why trash in oceans is very harmful, they distribute cleaning tools, we pick up trash, then place the collected trash in the nearby disposal site. The dirtiness of each beach can be varied, some might have many arge garbage around, and some mostly have tiny pastic pieces. But regardless of how much trash you can collect, it's still good to clean up even a little bit.

One of the things I really enjoy is the view, it's very peaceful to watch the ocean waves and the clear sky. There's one time I went to a beach during low tides, there's so much land you can walk around, and the sea looks very pretty. I also got to see some little creatures, like hermit crabs, crabs, sea cockroaches?

A little important thing is that you should monitor your health! One time I brought 1L of water, but I got exhausted quickly because of my heart palpitations (and also the fact I ran to the venue for I was almost late), and combined with the fact I had to climb stairs out of the beach, I swear I nearly passed out. Remember to rest in a cool area if you don't feel well!

Otherwise, beach cleaning has been a pretty nice experience, it is a good way to distract myself from how defeated I felt job hunting! Now, I wanna sleep, I woke up 6am this morning...
