Content Warning: Throwing Up

Looking at my Pocari Sweat sports drink, I suddenly got reminded of the time where I got a really bad food poisoning... And I'm surprised to see I never told the story here! So no better time to talk about it than now!

Two years ago, I had dinner at a MiXian (Rice Noodle) restaurant, I often go there, probably the same order of tamato soup mixian with pork and enoki mushrooms. I went home, took a shower and went to bed.

Suddenly, I was woken up with an urge to throw up, so I rushed to the toilet and did do. Huh, that's weird, time to go back to bed... Few minutes later, I threw up again, then again, then again, even after I threw everything up. This is bad, I thought to myself, and took some stomach pills, except they did nothing because I threw them up before it could even take effect.

The next day is a school day, it would be more than reasonable to take a day off. However, it was mid-terms week, there was a test the next day. While I could still take a sick leave and take the test another day, I was like "It's too much of a pain to postpon my test, I studied too long for this." So I just prayed I get better before the morning, and unfortunately, the pain and suffering continued.

If you want to imagine my situation back then, I was laying at the sofa because it's quicker to get to the toilet there, I had my water bottle with me but every time I take a sip, I throw them back out. I laid down on the sofa, staring at the clock, waiting for the nausea to go away. I was exhausted and dehydrated, I felt like I was dying. None of my family members were at home at the time, so nobody could even help me. On hindsight, I should've called an ambulence, but man I didn't want to cause trouble.

Anyways, it was morning, my condition had improved by a small margin. I could at least leave home to buy the Pocari Sweat sports drink, but despite how close the convenience store was, it felt like walking a mile. Anyways, I managed to drink the sports drink without throwing up, so I could say it may have saved my life. Not sponsored, but I have my eternal gratitude towards it.

I also needed to wait for the clinics to open, which I realise is difficult to find, as most gets filled up with people very quickly, with the fastest queue needing to wait until 10am. And because how exhausted I was, I didn't walk back home (it's rather close to my home), I just asked the clinic I had to lie down and fell asleep on the bench.

Anyways, got diagnosed with food poisoning, got some medicine and went back to rest at home (man the walk was painful). I got suffient rest to prepare for the test, which I performed quite well!

Lesson is that if you are sick, seriously, find a doctor and rest at home, don't be like me.
