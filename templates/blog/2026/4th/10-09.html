I recently came with a new idea about a new worldbuilding, separate from any of my current OCs and other stories, yes not even related to UtSS. It's a pretty big change cuz I spent a long time fixated on UtSS. It's basically a virtual game world and players are stuck in it, totally original.

The inspiration

This idea originated from me watching an anime called Goodnight World. It is about a group of players in a clan, they hang out and care for each other like a found family... But what they don't know is that they are family members in real life. And in real life, the family is very fallen apart, the protagonist is a shut in and doesn't like to talk to his family members, and he especially despises his father. I wasn't very interested in the virtual game world concept as first, but the horror part quickly hooked me in.

And one thing that intrigued me was when one of the antagonists confronted the protagonist. She is a notorious guild leader who causes trouble for fun, she has questionable fashion choices (her pirate outfit is stupidly revealing), and has a crush on the protagonist. Despite her reputation in the game, I really love how her instance on living in real life contrasts with the protagonist. A bit of spoilers: The protagonist greatly despises reality, he'd rather live in this virtual world forever. And the girl, who I would hate if she existed in real life, actually encourages him to touch grass, and use the game as a support to go through stress in real life. An antagonist with a mindset that matches the society standards is kinda funny, and that arc is genuinely interesting, but Pico why would you fall for the protagonist.

Major spoilers: But suddenly there's a twist, when the girl went somewhere, she met the creator of the game, also the protagonist's father. Then the father proceeded to say she is an AI all along, a brain scan clone from her real life counterpart. The guy then beats the shit out of the girl, and even burning her til her death.... WHERE THE FUCK DID THAT TWIST COME FROM???? The anime was going so well about family drama and over-reliance with gaming, and out of now where the AI shit came up, like??? TLDR I got disappointed by the twist and dropped the anime, maybe there's more that makes the twist better, but I'm not the type with patience.

Connecting some dots

Perhaps due to my immense disappointment, I couldn't stop thinking about that anime. And when thinking, I suddenly got reminded by my old OC concepts: It was back way before Cosmos was created, Lavender was still called Yume and Mira was not dead.

One day, a girl from the modern world found herself in a strange world, dreamlike and full of magic. She couldn't remember anything but she knew she didn't belong in this world. She then found a strange wizard called Yume, the keyholder to the gates which leads back to reality. However, Mira eventually realises she despised her real life, and didn't want to go back, I mean why not stay in the world where things come true? Unfortunately, the magical world's life is soon coming to an end, if she doesn't leave, she will collapse along with this world.

While I probably wasn't thinking about it, it is likely inspired from The Childish Girl and the Grown-Up World, it was one of my first exposure to voice bank songs, ahh my childhood... This is a song about a woman who was tired of her adulthood, and she founds a hole where she could be a kid again. While things went well at first, but due to a lack of management, thing spiraled and lifes were lost. In order to prevent that, they decided to establish laws and rules, and slowly it turned into a world similar to the one she hated: Full of work and lack of fun. She woke up in the real world, and then realised if the dangerous things were never created, the world can be safe and childlike forever. She dived into the hole again, but in reality it is a sewer in construction. While she may be dead in our POV, perhaps she successfully built. her perfect world, and need not to return.

There's also the fact I hated my life back then, and I often dreamt about a world where I am more powerful and magic is real. I often thought about a story about escapism, and debated in my head if someone with a miserable life got to live in a happier but fake world, would it be wrong for them to stay forever?

Ok back the the current times, I had the idea: If I combined this new inspiration with my old ideas, perhaps I can restart the concept as a new story!

The concept

In the recent age, a virtual online game has been viral around the world. With just a simple (expensive) headset, you can too enter the world of sandbox, PVP and all sorts of party games.

So there is the antagonist, who started out as a troll/griefer. They spammed chatboxes, destroyed creations by others, a genaral menace. Even if they get banned, they somehow came back in alts, the moderation system sure sucked. Then there was the game tournament, mixed with all minigames + PVP. And not only that, there was a group of players, way more obnoxious and malicious than the antagonist. Wow I have more standards than you, the antagonist said. They joined the tournament (which they haven't revealed their true identity as a troll yet) and managed to beat up the group in the tournament. And with that, they earned a bit of reputation within the community.

And there's the protagonist, who mostly spent their game time under the radar. Hearing about the tournament, the protag decided to join as well, and eventually got matched against the antagonist. Hearing about the antagonist's prior battle, they especially got excited to compete against them. While the antagonist is strong, the protagonist has practiced the game for a long time. It was a tough battle, and many people online got invested in the tournament and epecially the battle between those two. In the end, the protagonist won, earning various prizes including the cape, which is exclusive for the best players. But whether who won, it forever changed the two.

The antagonist eventually revealed themselves as a troll, while a lot of people were appalled, most of them decided to forgive them, you know, epic villain redemption, it was not without a temp ban though. But after returning, they decided to tone down their trolling and start a guild. While they could start anew in a new account, they found something special in this identity, fame they never expected, bonds they made in the guild, it is something they couldn't easily obtain.

For the protagonist, their awe is quite similar. They get greeted with fans in the game, they get to wear the cape which many desired, they see themselves drawn with the antagonist in a ship art. Life has never been this wonderful for the protagonist, it is more than what they ever dreamt of. They looked into their headset, if only life can be as good as this...

Upon this announcement, players went into a panic. What about their lives? What about their responsibilities? Hours turned into days, and days turned into weeks. Players realised the issue might not be fixed anytime soon, and started thinking of ways to deal with the situation. Perhaps, they can do something about the bug, maybe they can find a way to return to the real world! But for some, they might be slowly realising that being in the game forever isn't too bad?
