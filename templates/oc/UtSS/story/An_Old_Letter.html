“Mira had always been cheerful when she was little. Of course, no kid is ever willing to obediently sit through hours of lessons. But Mira? She’s clever as much as she’s playful, one time she launched herself out of the window with a pair of propellor shoes she made herself, surprising that she ended up unscathed.

Though she was a pain to keep an eye on, she has the infectious joy that cheers up anyone here, even the kids who keep themselves distant found themselves happy around Mira. In this era, this kind of optimism is a rare sight to see. And every time she dives into my arms for a hug, seeing her gigantic grin, I wished in my head that she would forever stay this way.

But eventually, Mira had to grow up. She became more independent, went outside a lot more, and started to make more friends. I know it’s a part of life to change, but it felt more drastic, and perhaps too sudden.

One early morning, I saw her outside the field. Mira sometimes likes to wake up early for her adventures, but that day she was just sitting on the ground watching the sunrise so quietly. Before I said anything, she simply invited me to sit beside her, as if she already knew I was behind her.

Her gaze, despite looking towards the golden sky, lacks the brightness that she used to have. The usual smile was missing from her face, replaced with an empty expression that I’m not used to seeing. I asked if anything is wrong, but she simply smiled and said everything is alright, it’s the first time I saw her fake a smile like that.

Something may have happened while I was away, but it was as if she aged decades in a span of a few days. I didn’t want to push her, so I simply talked about lighthearted things, like hobbies and reminiscing about the past. Her eyes remained fixated onto the distance, absentmindly nodding to every event I recalled.

After a while, she leaned onto my arm, which reassured me a bit. She mumbled, “Do you think that one day this will finally end?” I was confused what she meant by that, she then clarified, “Do you think the world will be peaceful again, like in the stories you tell me?” There’s something strange with the way she said it, perhaps she’s gotten weary about the problems of the land, but I told her that as long as we try, we can make the world better again.

When the sun fully rose, I learnt that Mira had went on an adventure, to study how the Voided Lands appeared. It has been a few months, and she had already discovered many historic artifacts and information that might help us lessen the withering. She’s a strong kid, I always knew she’d accomplish something great. But I hope no matter what happens, her heart will forever stay pure.”
