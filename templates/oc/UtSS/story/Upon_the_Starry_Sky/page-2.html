The two walked closely in the silent woods, Lavender paced towards the overgrown path, while Cosmos followed slightly behind, looking somewhat nervous. She asked the cloaked figure in front, “Where are you going? Isn’t it dangerous outside?”

“You will understand soon.” Lavender’s gaze remained forward, slowly maneuvering down the unsteady path, “Moreover, this may be a vital key to saving the world, so please cooperate.”

“So you have decided!” Cosmos cheered before clearing her throat, “While it might have been explained long ago, there’s no harm in explaining the Comet Guardian again!” She then spread out her wings, beginning her well-practiced speech, “As the duty of the Creator and the Hero, their job is to protect the world with the powers of us two combined!”

Cosmos raised her left paw, “A human is capable of magic, but their magic capacity is low, they run out of energy quickly, and their strength is limited.” 

She raised her other paw, “A Cometling contains a massive amount of magic essence, perhaps even with unimaginable powers! But they can’t use magic at all.” 

Cosmos then clasped her paws together, “But with the power of the Bond, their powers shall combine: A magic wielder with infinite powers! And with this power, the Comet Guardian can protect the world from the evil.”

“From evil, huh…” Lavender mumbled, then asked, “If me and you were to join powers, how long does this stay?” Cosmos tilted her head, “As long as the world needs saving, why do you ask?”

Before Cosmos realised, they have already reached the destination: The edge of Stardust Isle. While the Voided Lands always looked barren, the area they arrived at looked strangely empty. While no danger can hide behind trees and bushes, the lack of any flora around makes Cosmos slightly uneasy. 

And at the end of the land, there was nothing but the boundless sky and the deep nothingness. Cosmos noticed, “It’s the Borders… I expected it would take longer to reach here…” Cosmos flew to the edge of the lands, and put her paw on the invisble wall. “I knew that it exists, but seeing it in person… It still feels surreal!”

Cosmos turned her head back, “So, why did you bring me here?” Cosmos looked at Lavender, who was still standing a bit far away. As if Lavender got into a trance, she stood in place, gazing onto this area with a sorrowful look.

“Lavender, you ok?” Cosmos went closer to Lavender. 

“…I’m alright.” Lavender blinked.

“Cosmos, in order to turn the world back to normal, I will need your power.” Lavender stood on the wasteland, with her hand out, “How do I enact the Bond?”

“I guess this is the time!” Cosmos smiled, as she ruffled her wings, grabbing a feather out, “To establish the Bond, you just need my feather, and your signature. And after that, you will gain the powers of the Comet Guardian, and bear the burden to save the world from disaster.” Lavender tilted her head, “Only a signature…?”

“So, Lavender, “ Cosmos’ eyes glowed a vibrant teal light, an aura of magic manifested around them, she held the feather towards the human, “Will you take on this new power, and work with me to change this world for the better?”

“…Yes.” Lavender replied, taking the feather into her hands. A trail of light followed the tip of the feather, akin to ink. Lavender signed her name in the air…

A silent moment passed...

“…Nothing happened?” Lavender looked around, “Is this supposed to happen?”

Cosmos’ reaction seemed to be to no different, “Huh…? The Bond is… not valid?”

Lavender paced towards the confused Cometling, “What do you mean… Is there a criteria on who the Comet Guardian can be?”

“No… I mean someone else has signed the Bond with me already…”

“The Creator?”

“It’s not the Creator… It’s someone…” Cosmos held her hands on her head, “I don’t remember…”

“Can’t you cancel it?”

“No, I don’t think-“

Lavender grabbed Cosmos, “Then what do I do now?!” Cosmos faced the glare of Lavender yet again, it’s a mix of anger, desperation… sadness? 

“Why do you suddenly want my power anyways?” Cosmos asked.

Without warning, a loud tremble shook the land. Unable to steady herself, Lavender fell as her grip on Cosmos lossened, letting the Cometling escape.

Sounds of rumbles and thunder echoed all around, and suddenly, a huge crack appeared underneath, the ground breaking into pieces. 

“The energy from that beam… It’s still here?!” Lavender realised.

Before they had the time to react, Lavender fell into the cracks.

“LAVENDER!!” Cosmos panicked, as she flew with all her energy into the hole.

“…avender, are you ok?!”

Lavender opened her eyes, it appeared she ended up somewhere under the ground. And beside her is the Cometling, which glowed brightly in the cave. Looking at the surroundings, it seemed escaping this place would be difficult.

“Thank goodness you are awake, it’s lucky you seem unhurt!” Cosmos looked around, “Don’t worry, I will get you out… Somehow!”

As Cosmos searched for any paths or route to go back up, Lavender simply stared at her. “…Why are you trying to help me?”

Cosmos dumbfoundedly looked back, “Why wouldn’t I?”

Lavender sat with her legs bent, resting her head and arms on her knees, “You shouldn’t, there are better hero candidates out there.”

Cosmos scolded, “Hero or not, I won’t just abandon someone in need to their death!”

“Even if the person is responsible for the Hero’s death?”

The Cometling froze in her tracks, “What?” Cosmos stared at Lavender, whose face is buried into her legs, “What do you mean, responsible for the Hero’s death, the Hero is-“

“The Hero is immortal, but with an exception. You know that already, right?” Lavender murmured. “With the existence of a Hero, there has to be the opposite counterpart. And that, is her weakness.”

Cosmos was reminded a piece of an important memory. A role tied to the Hero…

“…However, the world was far from perfect, and evil still lurked within. Shrouded with jealousy, they sabotaged the Creator, attempting to claim the world for their own.”

It’s a role eventually became redundant to the Creator, or at least for the past… Cosmos looked at Lavender, strangely uninjured from the long fall. It couldn’t be just coincidence, the person she has been talking to all along is…

“…The Villain?” Cosmos muttered.

“Protect the world from the evil, huh? Now’s it’s the perfect time to seal it here for good.” The not-so-ordinary human laughed bitterly, “…Just go, it’s better off for me this way.”

But instead, Lavender felt a paw on her hand.

“I… don’t know what happened between you and the Hero…” Cosmos admitted, “But whatever it was, I knew you were close with her, and you don’t actually want her to die.”

Lavender remained silent.

Cosmos spoke with reassurance, “Even if you are the Villain, I don’t sense any malice from you.”

The human gritted her teeth, but still refused to look up, “So you are just basing everything on feelings now? What about the who saving the world plan you care so much about?”

Cosmos’ flicked her ears as she heard another distant echo of strange noise. She grabbed Lavender by the arm, panickedly trying to drag her out.

“No matter who you are, I would never abandon anyone in need!“ Cosmos exclaimed as she tried to fly them both out of the hole, but her wings seemed to be too weak.

“Just let me be, Mira died because of me! The world is better off-“

“The Hero is not dead!” Cosmos yelled.

“What?” Lavender’s eyes widened. 

“When either the Hero or Villain dies, the world undergoes a change… It will revert into its initial form, restarting the story back then the Hero was first active!”

Cosmos’ gaze was determined, “Mira is still out there somewhere! We can find her together!”

For the first time, Cosmos saw this expression on the human, her eyelids fluttered, her eyes glittered like hope shining into her heart after so, so many years. Without a word, Lavender hand firmly held onto Cosmos, finally standing up.

Cosmos flapped her wings, “Alright, there’s nothing we can’t do together!” Lavender concernedly asked, “How do we even leave here? It’s not like we can use magic…”

But Lavender noticed a change, it’s as if Cosmos was shining even brighter, her body grew larger, her form finally reassembling the ancient drawings, a blue-yellow cat with big wings. An aura of magic manifested around them, Lavender felt a breeze of cool wind blowing.

“This is…!” Cosmos stared at her body in awe, she then inhaled deeply, as she whispered to herself, “Ok, we’ve got this… on the count of three…”

1… The two focused upwards.

2… Cosmos stretched her wings out.

3…

Suddenly, the magic powers rushed through them, a giant burst of energy pushed them upwards. Their bodies were protected by a barrier of magic, carrying them out all the way to the surface.

When Lavender opened her eyes, she realised she was in the sky.

“AHHH CAREFUL!” Cosmos caught Lavender by her vest before she started free falling. Cosmos seemed to struggle with grabbing things with her new form, “I wish I had hands…!”

The process was clumsy, but in the end they safely got carried to the ground.

“You didn’t have to carry me, it’s not like it would hurt me.” Lavender sat down on the ground.

“It would still hurt, no?” Cosmos lied down flat on the floor, panting from exhaustion. “Anyways, it was so cool when you used that magic, we got out thanks to you!”

Lavender tilted her head, “What? No, the magic came from you.”

“What do you mean? Cometlings can’t use magic!”

“Cosmos, I couldn’t use magic long ago. And that type of magic, it’s definitely yours.”

…

“So,” Cosmos clasped her paws, “The Creator left this world, the new Hero is missing, and I…”

Cosmos inhaled, “Can somehow use magic??”

“It appears that way…” Lavender continued jotting down the key points, trying to ignore the mess in the house after the sudden earthquake. “I haven’t told you this, but days before you woke up, there was also a beam of energy shooting into the sky from where I found you.”

“Ugh, I can’t follow it all…” Sitting on the table, Cosmos put her paws on her head, frowning. Lavender wrote down as she spoke, “I assume you didn’t know anything about that either…”

Before Lavender finished writing the sentence, something appeared in her brain. “Hey, when you said ‘The world reverts to its initial form after the Hero or Villain dies’… What does that mean…?”

The Cometling’s shoulders tightened, she opened her mouth, but quickly calmed down, she spoke, “When the Creator created this world, she wished to immerse herself in her own creation.”

Cosmos looked into her paws, “That design… It was to make sure the Creator gets to start over, try better choices, learning new things, and also to protect her from permanent harm…“ She lowered her head, “But I don’t remember anything past the Creator leaving… I’m sorry for hiding so many things…”

Lavender stared at the Cometling, that answer seemed to have brought more questions than answers, but after the whole ordeal outside… “It’s fine.” Lavender sighed, “Moreover, I shouldn’t have been so harsh to you back then.”

“…Sorry.” Lavender murmured, looking away from Cosmos.

Cosmos raised her ears. Staring at Lavender, she smiled softly.

The Cometling then lied down on the table, “So, what’s your plan now?”

Lavender looked at the map placed next to her notes, “If we were to find information about Mira, the best place would be the Tower of Divine.” Lavender held her pen tighter, frowning, “But what will they do if they see me?”

“Don’t worry, when there is a will, there is a way!” Cosmos tapped Lavender’s shoulders, “A path of hardships is which all Heroes go through…”

Cosmos felt a dumbfounded stare from the “Hero”, the Cometling stumbled her words, “W…Well, even you’re not the Hero set by the world, you can still try to do something, right? And I will be right here by your side, helping you every step of the way!”

That optimism, it brought a sense of strange nostalgia to Lavender, she finished writing new found information on her notebook, “I will make up for everything, Mira.” Then closing it with Cosmos’ feather as a bookmark.

Within the forest, a mysterious woman in a robe walked along with a winged cat walking besides. The path was shrouded with obstacles, and the path on the map had long been altered. But even then, they might be able to find their destination eventually…
