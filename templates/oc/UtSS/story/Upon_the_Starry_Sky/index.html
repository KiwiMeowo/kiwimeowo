“Once upon a time, the Creator, along with her Cometling, created Stardust Isle. She built the land, the people and the stories. 

It’s a world of magic, it’s a world of art, the people of Stardust Isle could express themselves with their powers. However, the world was far from perfect, and evil still lurked within. So the Creator landed onto this world, and defeated the evil. Eventually, the world was given peace, a happy ending for everyone.”

At least, this is the story the Creator wrote, and what the Cometling first remembered.

Somewhere in the distant woods, a lone wanderer stumbled onto a strange looking rock, or perhaps, a gemstone. Before she could properly examine, the gemstone started cracking.

crack

“Hello human!” A little winged creature manifested from the gemstone, its body emitted a gentle blue glow. “I am here to guide you into the world of magic!”

With the sudden appearance, the human stumbled backwards, blankly staring at the strange cat? Thing? While it resembled a cat, it’s really tiny, and its body was somewhat ethereal.

The cometling examined the person in front of her: A lady in a robe, she has light violet hair with bangs that could cover her face, her deep purple eyes still fixated on the Cometling.

“Oh don’t be afraid Hero, I am not a dangerous individual.” The tiny creature waves its even tinier paws, “I am Cosmos, the Cometling that will be your companion from today on!”

“…Cometling?” The person furrowed her eyebrows, “Hero?”

“Yes, as the Hero of this world, your fate is to protect-“

“You must be mistaken then, I am not the person you are looking for.” The human finally stood up, patting off the debris on her clothes.

“H… huh?? Hero, what do you mean-“

“The Hero is long dead, Cometling.”

“Dead?” Cosmos stopped confused, it was only then she started to look around the surroundings, and whatever environment she had imagined being in, it wasn’t this: A grey, barren forest, or at least the remnants of it. It’s like there was an explosion where she stood, a circle of charred ground and grass, with trees only present a distance away. There was not a sound or rustle except for the two only living beings here., and the distant echoes of rumbling, “Wait… Something’s wrong…”

The human contemplate for a short moment, and pointed at a distance, “I cannot help you with your quest, but there are other people who may help you.” The human explained. “Sorry for the inconvenience, but I must go.”

As she turned around, Cosmos frantically reached out her paws, “W… Wait…!” The creature stumbled her words, “I don’t know what happened before I woke up, but I know it isn’t too late to save the world!”

“Perhaps *you* are the Hero who will save the world!”

Cosmos then was greeted with a glare. Not a look of confusion, disbelief, or even ridicule. It was anger, even if it’s a split second, Cosmos froze in place.

The human composed herself, “I know all these information is new to you, but even if the Hero is, somehow, still out there,” She turned her head back to the direction she’s heading, “It definitely isn’t me.” The human walked into a path, disappearing into the darkness.

“Why are you still following me?” The human
spoke inside of her house. “Then why did you just leave me? I still don’t understand anything!” Cosmos replied, sitting outside of the door, her tail whacking on the ground.

The house the human is living in is quite worn down, the walls are full of cracks, the plants are overgrown, and even the door feels like it would fail to close properly. Cosmos wondered if the human isn’t that good at house maintenance, or if she even bothered to do so.

“You are the Cometling, so you should’ve known what happened already.” Replied from inside the house, “Besides, if you do have questions, you can just find others instead.” Cosmos sighed, barely just existed, and ended up like this.

“But you’re the one who summoned me, ” Cosmos bargained, “You would be the one who knows the most.” There’s no response from the door.

Cosmos asked anyway, “What happened before you created the world?”

Finally, there’s a response. “Pardon?” The Cometling paused, “…Huh?” The human’s interest suddenly rose, “What do you mean *I* created the world?”

“Did’t you summon me after creating this world?” Cosmos questioned. The human replied surprisingly quickly, “I simply picked up the artifact containing you.”

While Cosmos was still processing her confusion, the human elaborated, “According to the legends, the Creator had left the world along with the Cometling ages ago. But at least the part about the Cometling may be wrong, considering you are still here.”

It seems something had finally clicked, Cosmos asked one more question, “So… you are just a normal civilian here?” The human answered, “…I guess so.”

Cosmos froze still, “Well… Just forget about what I’ve said, haha…” She nervously chuckled. “Oh by the way, what’s your name?”

“It’s Lavender.” The human was starting to be cooperative.

“Ooh nice name! Are you interested in flowers and such?”

“My name isn’t related to my interests, but I do study herbs and medicine for a bit.”

“I see!!! So are you a medic of sorts?”

“…I actually have a question for you, “ This time the human initiated a topic. Cosmos peaked her ears excitedly, “Yes?”

“What’s the connection between the Creator and the Hero?”

Even through the door, the human could sense a jolt from Cosmos, “OH UH, WELL, ACTUALLY NO I MEAN WHATSACREATOR I MEAN UHHHH-“

“Nooo???” Cosmos is bad at lying.

After a moment of awkwardness, the human opened the door, she has removed her robe, showing her light-violet colored braids. “Let’s talk.”

“So the Creator… is supposed to be the Hero?” Lavender thought aloud, while writing some notes on the table. Cosmos peered over the table, but the handwriting was impossible for her to understand.

“I’m not supposed to tell this to anyone else but… yes.” Cosmos lowered her head, ashamed how bad she is at keeping secrets. “Most people shouldn’t have known about the Creator either…” 

The human corrected, “The legend about the Creator is actually a well-known story on this land, nobody doesn’t know it... But the Hero is someone else.”

“And that’s what I’m confused about…” The Cometling crossed her arms. As Cosmos processed the new information, she looked around the house. While it’s quite messy, it’s probably just organised clutter. Labelled jars of herbs and potions stood inside the cabinets, stacks of paper laid across the desks. That corner of the room were perhaps the messiest part, with layers of clothing simply placed on the floor…

“Oh, is that a witch hat?” Cosmos pointed, “Are you a witch?”

Lavender continued writing her notes, not lifting her head, “There is a difference between a witch and a wizard. While both studies magic, witches focus on external magic, such as potions and medicine, while wizards focus on the magic within themselves.”

“Ahh, so are you a wizard?” Cosmos asked. Lavender’s hands paused suddenly, but quickly resumed, “No, not anymore.” 

“Oh…” As if to avoid another awkward silence, Cosmos started a new topic, “Anyways, what did you know about the Hero you told me about?”

For a moment Lavender’s gaze moved away somewhere, as if reliving a long-distant memory. “There was once a girl, who they call Miracle.”

“Living in a mundane village, she one day decided to venture out into the outer world to uncover the history of this land, and to find a solution to stop the world from the Void.”

“Voids…?” Cosmos enquired. Lavender pointed out of the window, it seems there is a border between a somehow regular looking forest, and an area fully tainted with dark substance. “We are in Voided Lands, the highly-infected area of Voids. Under its effect, plants rot, creatures die, life would fail to thrive.”

Like perfect timing, a section of trees fell in the distance, the mugs on the table shivered at the sound. Cosmos tail stood up, “Wha?? Then why are you still living here?”

Lavender continued the story, “With her efforts, people got to see some of the artifacts left behind by the Creator, such as writings, drawings, and various legendary flora and fauna.” Lavender stood up, reached into one of the shelves, taking out a map, “Eventually, she recuited a team of intelligent wizards and witches, called the Sky Pioneers. Located in the center of the Stardust Isle, they studied ways to cure the Void within the Tower of the Divine.”

Lavender laid down the map onto the table, “With her accomplishments, she started to be referred as the Hero. The one figure mentioned in the legends, the one who will save the world from demise.”

“Oh hey, that’s the place where I first greeted my partner!” Cosmos pointed at where the tower is. “Partner?” Lavender asked.

“Oh yea, that’s how I call the Creator!” Cosmos nodded quickly, “Ahh, so I did lose my memories! I wonder why though…”
“So what happened next?” Cosmos put her paws on the table, anticipating for the next part. Lavender turned her face away, “Miracle later then passed away.” Cosmos furrowed her brows “…How?”

Lavender looked down for a moment, and replied, “They weren’t sure, but she one day entered the Voided Lands, and never returned. ” As if sensed a hint of sorrowness within those words, Cosmos tried to peer at Lavender’s expression.

Lavender let a breathe out, “…But this shouldn’t be how it ends, right?” Lavender’s gaze was determined, “Is there any way to bring her back?”

Cosmos’ eyes darted away, “I…I don’t really know if that’s possible…” She murmured while trying to recompose herself, “The Hero isn’t supposed to die anyways…” When Cosmos turned back her eyes, she seemed to caught a glimse of Lavender seemingly… distressed?

“In what way?” Lavender questioned, her gaze fixed onto Cosmos. Cosmos closed her eyes, contemplating something, and finally replied, “It’s not something I should reveal, but I don’t think hiding it will be helpful anymore…”

“The Hero… is supposed to be immune from all harm.” Cosmos reciprocated Lavender’s eye contact, her expression firm.

“I see…” Lavender replied while sitting back down, her hands on her chin, pondering something with a serious look.

Cosmos’ voice became apologetic, “But what I mean is, while I’m not saying Miracle isn’t necessarily lying, for me it just… doesn’t make sense, is all…” Lavender nodded while facing the other way, “I know it’s strange, but the circumstances now is different.” She stared at a particular notebook, “Mira never got injured during adventures, that’s all I can say now.” 

“Mira?” Cosmos raised an eyebow. “…Miracle.” Lavender corrected.

Lavender furrowed her brows, then as if an idea popped in her head, she asked, “What power do you use?” Cosmos raised her ears, “Oh I don’t use magic, but when my powers and a human’s combine, we would become a Comet Guardian!” 

Cosmos then widened her eyes, excitedly exclaimed, “Oh are you interested in my offer?!” Lavender questioned, “I thought you’d reserve your powers for the Creator, or the Hero.”

Cosmos thought aloud, “Since I’ve been away from the Creator for a long while, the Bond has already been cancelled…” Cosmos turned her eyes back onto Lavender, her eyes sparkling like the stars, “While you might not be the Hero, I still think you still have the potential to save the world!” Lavender squinted, “Why so?” Cosmos chuckled, “Fate of course! You are the first human I met!” Lavender sighed.

Cosmos continued, “Back on topic, I don’t think the powers include bringing back the dead, but I know it can manipulate objects…” Surprisingly, Lavender became intrigued, “Objects?”

“You know, the earth, the air, the sea. The Comet Guardian can alter their behaviour and shapes.” Cosmos elaborated.

“Then how about magic essense?” Lavender asked yet again. 

“Pretty sure it works as well!” Cosmos confidently answered.

Upon hearing the reply, Lavender stood up again, grabbing some items from the corner of the room. “Let’s go outside.”
